Common Job Scam Warning Signs

Be alert to these red flags when reviewing job listings:

  • Unrealistically high salaries with minimal qualifications
  • Poor grammar and spelling errors
  • Unverified company information
  • Generic or vague job descriptions
  • Requests for upfront payments or personal info
[Icon]

Salary Red Flags

Be suspicious of unusual salary offers:

  • Salaries 50%+ higher than market rate
  • Vague or extremely wide salary ranges
  • Promises of unrealistic signing bonuses
  • Currency mismatches or unclear payment terms
  • No salary information provided
[Icon]

Verification Tips

How to verify job listings:

  • Check company's official website and LinkedIn
  • Call company directly using official number
  • Research the hiring manager on LinkedIn
  • Verify email addresses match company domain
  • Check Glassdoor and Indeed reviews
[Icon]

Communication Red Flags

Watch for suspicious communication:

  • Pressure to apply or respond immediately
  • Communication only via email or messaging
  • Grammatically poor responses from recruiters
  • Requests to communicate outside official channels
  • Unsolicited job offers without application
[Icon]

Payment & Personal Info Scams

Never provide payment or sensitive details:

  • Legitimate jobs never ask for upfront fees
  • Avoid giving SSN before employment offer
  • No direct deposit setup before first day
  • Beware of "advance" checks for work-from-home
  • Verify wire transfer requests through company
[Icon]

What to Do If Scammed

Steps if you've encountered a job scam:

  • Report to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov
  • Report to the job platform (Indeed, LinkedIn, etc)
  • Contact the impersonated company
  • Monitor credit for identity theft
  • Save all communications for evidence
