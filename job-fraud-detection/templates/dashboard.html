About FraudGuard

FraudGuard is dedicated to protecting job seekers from fraudulent job listings through advanced machine learning analysis. Our mission is to create a safer job market by identifying scams before they can harm unsuspecting applicants.

How Our Detection Works

Feature Analysis

We analyze 8 key indicators including salary ranges, company verification, location specificity, description quality, contact verification, posting age, grammar quality, and keyword patterns.

Machine Learning

Our Gradient Boosting model identifies complex patterns that distinguish legitimate job postings from fraudulent ones with high accuracy.

Instant Detection

Based on feature analysis, the model classifies listings as Legitimate or Fraudulent with confidence scores for verification.
